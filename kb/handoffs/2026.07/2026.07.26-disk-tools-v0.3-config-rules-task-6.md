## Decisions Made During Implementation

- **Five reversals in the concept, not the two the spec named.** Reading §5 whole
  turned up three more: `deny_unknown_fields` (typos as errors) became a warning,
  `[scan] excludes` was dropped in favour of per-rule ones, and the `[report]`
  sketch was adjusted. A concept left describing terrain that does not exist is
  worse than no concept.
- **The concept is amended in place, not superseded.** §5 describes something now
  built; a reader arriving at it needs the correction there, not in a file they
  have to know to look for.
- **The architecture snapshot is a delta.** The 2026.07.25 one still describes the
  pipeline correctly, so this covers only what moved and says so in its first
  paragraph. Repeating the whole thing would leave two documents to keep in step.
- **`just install-cli` replaces `cargo install --path cli` in the README.** The
  recipe also checks which `disk-tools` is first on `PATH`, which is the failure
  this project has already hit once.
